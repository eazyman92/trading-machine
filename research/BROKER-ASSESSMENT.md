# Broker integration assessment

Research date: 3 November 2026. Scope: public documentation for the app's global stock, forex, gold, and oil ambitions, with Nigeria as the development/test location. This is a comparative desk review, not a completed broker reliability audit or confirmation that every resident can open an account. No broker accounts were opened, messages sent, credentials requested, deposits made, or orders placed.

## Recommendation from the evidence

Build a multi-broker capability model. Prioritize a cTrader Open API adapter qualification, IBKR for broad listed-market coverage, and Alpaca for eligible US-stock users. Compare regional forex routes before adding another adapter. Do not choose a universal winner or enable countries based solely on a broker brand being present there.

Nigeria remains a specific unresolved onboarding check. A country absent from a restriction list is not positive proof of eligibility. Eligibility to use a personal API is also not permission to operate a commercial app for unrelated clients.

## How a candidate becomes a recommended integration

Mandatory gates, before numerical ranking:

1. Confirm exact resident country, legal entity, account type, and target instruments; record dated evidence. Demo-account availability is not live-account approval.
2. Match legal entity and domain against the applicable current regulator register and permitted activity. A group license must not be assigned to every group's client.
3. Confirm commercial multi-user API terms, authorization/revocation flow, automation permission, and market-data usage/redistribution rights.
4. Check practical funding/withdrawal methods, currencies, fees, minimums, limits, and third-party payment restrictions for the specific country.
5. Run an authorized demo integration to measure p50/p95/p99 acknowledgement latency, disconnect recovery, event order, partial fills, cancel/replace races, rate limits, and reconciliation. Demo behavior cannot prove live slippage or withdrawal reliability.

Only gated candidates should receive weighted scores for user protection, API lifecycle correctness, target-market coverage, total cost, funding practicality, reliability evidence, and support. Do not fabricate scores for missing evidence or equate lower advertised spreads with lower realized costs.

Regulatory checks completed in this pass are limited. A CIRO December 2025 dealer list names Interactive Brokers Canada, OANDA Canada, and GAIN Capital–FOREX.com Canada; this is dated supporting evidence, not a November 2026 permission certificate. Pepperstone's group page describes different protections by entity. The Seychelles regulator directory could not be fetched successfully; Raw Trading's current register match remains unresolved. [CIRO list](https://www.ciro.ca/media/12811/download?inline=), [Pepperstone disclosures](https://pepperstonegroup.com/en/regulated-offices).

## Questions prepared for broker qualification

No outreach has been sent. Resolve through current official documentation or authorized contact:

- Can a Nigerian resident open the specific API-compatible account today? Which entity contracts with them?
- Can a Nigerian business operate a third-party app for the proposed resident countries? What approvals and agreements are needed?
- Which stock, forex, gold, and oil instruments are actually available through that account/API? Are these shares, CFDs, futures, or other products?
- Does the authorization model support unrelated clients without collecting their broker passwords? What trading/withdrawal scopes and revocation controls exist?
- What do market-data display, storage, historical testing, derived analytics, and redistribution cost and permit?
- What are the applicable order/connection rates, data throttling, client order identifiers, margin semantics, and order-status recovery guarantees?
- Which funding and withdrawal routes work for the target resident country, and what evidence supports service reliability?

## Research limits

All populated market and API facts above are from official vendor/regulator materials. Broker marketing performance claims were not accepted as measured latency. No jurisdiction is marked launch-ready, no current regulator register has been fully verified for every entity, and no broker has passed our demo, cost, or operational tests. Final regional recommendations depend on those results; unsupported countries should remain explicitly unavailable rather than being routed through an unsuitable account.
